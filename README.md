# Privacy Policy for CLEAR OUT

**Last Updated:** October 3, 2026

## 1. Overview

CLEAR OUT (“CLEAR OUT” or “the Game”) is developed by Mahmoud Abdel-Hafiz / MoudLabs.

CLEAR OUT is designed to operate with minimal collection of personal information. MoudLabs does not operate a CLEAR OUT account service, analytics service, advertising service, or custom multiplayer server.

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device and uses Apple services where necessary for online multiplayer.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

CLEAR OUT stores game information locally on your device so that the Game can remember your progress, settings, and records.

Locally stored information may include:

- Level progress
- Completed levels
- Best completion times
- Tap records
- Puzzle statistics
- Total play time
- Daily Puzzle results
- Streaks
- Endless records
- Duel win/loss/draw records
- Sound and haptic preferences
- Gameplay settings
- Your chosen Nearby Duel display name

This information is stored locally by the Game and is not uploaded to a MoudLabs server.

## 3. Nearby Duel

Nearby Duel allows two devices on the same or nearby local network to play against each other.

CLEAR OUT uses Apple networking technologies and Bonjour discovery to locate and communicate with nearby players. iOS may request permission for CLEAR OUT to access your local network before Nearby Duel can function.

During a Nearby Duel, the devices may exchange information required to operate the match, including:

- Your chosen Nearby Duel display name
- Game version information
- Selected difficulty
- Match configuration
- Puzzle information
- Readiness status
- Arrows remaining
- Lives remaining
- Completion information
- Match results

This information is exchanged directly between participating devices for the purpose of operating the match. MoudLabs does not receive or store this information on a remote server.

## 4. Game Center and Online Duel

CLEAR OUT uses Apple Game Center and GameKit for Online Duel.

Game Center may be used for:

- Authentication
- Matchmaking
- Quick Match
- Invitations
- Game Center player display names
- Real-time multiplayer communication

CLEAR OUT may access Game Center player information supplied by Apple that is necessary to display players and operate a multiplayer match.

The Game also uses Game Center scoped player identifiers transiently to coordinate a match. These identifiers are not stored in CLEAR OUT's local player history or uploaded to a MoudLabs server.

Game Center data and Game Center account information are otherwise managed by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy practices.

## 5. Multiplayer Gameplay Data

During a multiplayer session, participating devices may exchange information necessary to conduct and synchronize the Duel.

This can include:

- Match identifiers
- Puzzle seeds
- Board-validation information
- Difficulty choices
- Game configuration
- Readiness states
- Arrows remaining
- Lives remaining
- Completion times
- Finish or failure states
- Rematch choices
- Match results

This information is used only for multiplayer gameplay and is not used by MoudLabs for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

## 6. Analytics and Diagnostics

CLEAR OUT does not integrate a third-party analytics or telemetry service.

The Game may generate limited diagnostic messages using Apple's system logging facilities for troubleshooting networking and Game Center behavior. CLEAR OUT does not send these logs to a developer-operated analytics server.

Apple may separately provide developers with crash, diagnostic, or App Store usage information when users choose to share such information with developers through Apple's system settings and services.

## 7. Advertising and Tracking

CLEAR OUT does not display third-party advertising.

CLEAR OUT does not use advertising identifiers, does not perform cross-app tracking, and does not use App Tracking Transparency for advertising or tracking purposes.

MoudLabs does not sell personal information.

## 8. Third-Party Services

CLEAR OUT currently uses Apple-provided services and frameworks necessary for its features, including:

- **Apple Game Center / GameKit** for Online Duel
- **iOS networking technologies** for Nearby Duel

CLEAR OUT does not currently include third-party advertising, analytics, social-media, or telemetry SDKs.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress, records, statistics, settings, and your Nearby Duel display name remain stored locally on your device unless they are changed, reset where applicable, or the application's local data is removed.

Using CLEAR OUT's **Reset Progress** feature resets gameplay progress and records but preserves your game settings, including your Nearby Duel display name.

CLEAR OUT does not maintain a remote player account or server-side copy of this locally stored game data.

Game Center information is managed separately by Apple.

## 10. Security

CLEAR OUT minimizes the information exchanged during multiplayer and does not operate a centralized player-data server.

Nearby Duel information is transmitted directly between participating devices over the local network. Online Duel communication is handled through Apple's Game Center infrastructure.

No electronic communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Your Choices

You can play CLEAR OUT's single-player modes without creating a CLEAR OUT account.

You may choose whether to:

- Use Nearby Duel
- Permit local network access
- Use Online Duel through Game Center
- Choose a local display name for Nearby Duel

Game Center settings can be managed through Apple's system settings.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if CLEAR OUT's features or privacy practices change.

Any revision will be reflected by updating the **Last Updated** date above.
